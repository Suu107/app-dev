# 💧 My Favorite Character: **Rimuru Tempest**

> A slime who became the ruler of a monster nation.

---

## 📺 About the Series

***That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime*** is a fantasy *isekai* anime.

| Detail         | Info                     |
|----------------|--------------------------|
| Genre          | Isekai, Fantasy, Action  |
| Main Character | Rimuru Tempest           |

## 🧍➡️💧 Rimuru's Story

1. Satoru Mikami, an office worker, dies in Tokyo.
2. He is reborn in another world as a **slime**.
3. He builds a monster nation called **Jura-Tempest Federation**.

## ⚡ Rimuru's Skills

### 🌌 Ultimate Skills
- **Raphael, Lord of Wisdom** – super-fast thinking and analysis
- **Beelzebuth, Lord of Gluttony** – eats anything and steals its skills
- **Uriel, Lord of Vows** – powerful barriers and space control
- **Azathoth, God of the Void** – Raphael and Beelzebuth fused together
- **Shub-Niggurath, Lord of Harvest** – creates, copies and gives skills

### 🔮 Unique Skills
- **Great Sage** – analyzes and gives advice
- **Predator** – eats and copies abilities
- **Mimicry** – copies the forms of what he eats

### ✨ Extra Skills
- **Gravity Flight** – flies by controlling gravity
- **Demon Lord's Haki** – an aura that scares enemies
- **Infinite Regeneration** – heals from almost anything
- **Water Manipulation** – controls water
- **Universal Thread** – sticky threads for binding enemies
- **Future Attack Prediction** – sees attacks before they happen

### 🛡️ Resistances
- Physical Attack Nullification
- Pain Nullification
- Abnormal Condition Nullification
- Spiritual Attack Nullification

## ⭐ Why I Like Rimuru

He is **smart**, *kind*, and **funny** 😄.
He went from ~~weak~~ to **super overpowered**, and he gets ***serious when he needs to***.
